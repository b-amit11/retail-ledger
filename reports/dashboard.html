Retail Ledger

Sales, cancellations and observed customer retention · December 2010–December 2011 · GBP

Findings

  • Recorded gross sales GBP 10,642,110.80 less cancellation value GBP 893,979.73 equals net transaction value GBP 9,748,131.07.
  • Cancellation value is 8.40% of gross sales. It is not an item-return rate: cancellations are not matched to original purchases.
  • The UK accounts for 84.0% of net transaction value. Review international markets separately so the UK does not hide differences.
  • December 2011 stops on the 9th and is excluded from trend and cohort comparisons.
  • Cohorts begin at first observed purchase, not proven first-ever purchase. Customers without IDs are excluded from retention, but remain in transaction totals.

White cells are unobserved periods, not zero retention.

Method and source

SQL classifies transactions and aggregates source records. R calculates monthly changes and renders these charts. Exact duplicate rows are excluded as an explicit assumption. Nonpositive prices and non-cancellation negative adjustments are excluded from sales metrics and retained in the audit.

No cost, payment settlement, acquisition spend, or cancellation reason is available. This is not profit, realized cash, or causal marketing analysis.
